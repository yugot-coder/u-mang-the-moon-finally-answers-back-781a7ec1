# Candidates — The Moon Finally Answers Back

One line, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last
to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.
The house judge reads lines two at a time and picks the funniest one that
still sounds like the Moon said it.

## The ten candidates

1. I don't grant wishes. I just reflect sunlight and watch you all make the same mistakes in better lighting.
2. You keep wishing on me like I'm a vending machine. I am a rock. A very tired, very pretty rock.
3. Yes, I heard your 2 a.m. promise. No, I didn't believe it either.
4. Stop howling. I've been up here 4.5 billion years and not once has anyone howled in tune.
5. Every werewolf blames me. I just show up. You lot do the howling yourselves.
6. You wished on a star once. It told me. We're both still laughing.
7. Four billion years of listening, and the best wish so far was a parking space.
8. I'm not ignoring you. I'm 384,400 kilometres away and even I can hear you begging.
9. I've seen every one of your late-night promises. The Moon remembers. Your ex doesn't have to.
10. You call me beautiful every night and then blame me for the tides. Pick a side, darling.

## The pick

**Candidate 1** — 106 characters:

> I don't grant wishes. I just reflect sunlight and watch you all make the same mistakes in better lighting.

Why it wins:

- **Funny first, no explaining.** The joke is the premise itself — the
  Moon is not a wish-granter, just a mirror — and it lands in one read.
- **Sounds like the Moon.** "I just reflect sunlight" is literally what
  the Moon does; only the Moon could say it, so the voice is unmistakable.
- **Twist in the tail.** "in better lighting" turns a cosmic shrug into a
  punchline: humanity's failures, now elegantly lit.
- **Answers the brief directly.** It replies to the wishes themselves —
  the core of the task — rather than to one narrow habit like howling.
- **Hemat kata, membekas.** 106 characters, three short beats, nothing
  wasted; the line is quotable the way a comeback should be.

## Set aside

- Candidate 2: strong voice ("a very tired, very pretty rock") but the
  vending-machine setup takes a beat too long to reach the rock.
- Candidate 3: punchy, but the 2 a.m. promise is only one of the three
  things the Moon is answering; narrower than the pick.
- Candidate 4: the "not once in tune" gag is good, but howling alone
  leaves the wishes unanswered.
- Candidate 5: funny, but blaming the werewolves lets the wishers off
  the hook — the pick keeps the whole of humanity in frame.
- Candidate 6: short and sharp, but "we're both still laughing" needs
  the reader to supply the star's voice; the pick is self-contained.
- Candidate 7: the parking-space wish is a great image, yet it is a
  one-gag line; the pick has a premise and a twist.
- Candidate 8: the distance number is a nice Moon fact, but "begging"
  is meaner than funny and the line has no turn.
- Candidate 9: "The Moon remembers" is a good voice, but the ex joke
  narrows the audience to the recently dumped.
- Candidate 10: "Pick a side, darling" is a fine closer, but blaming
  the Moon for the tides is a complaint, not an answer to the wishes.

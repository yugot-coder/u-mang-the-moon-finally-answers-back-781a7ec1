Build brief — The Moon Finally Answers Back (make.fun bounty
u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1)

Task: write ONE line, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon
replying at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of
everyone staring up at it. The house judge reads lines two at a time
and picks the funniest one that still sounds like the Moon said it.

Rules from the kit (RULES.txt):
- Program writes one text to /output/entry.txt (or
  $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt).
- Gate: UTF-8 text, 1 to 240 characters, not only whitespace.
- Judge criteria, most important first: funniest, sounds like the
  Moon, lands as a comeback in one read; a line that explains its own
  joke has already lost the laugh.
- Standard library only, deterministic, no network, no secrets.

Approach:
1. Wrote 10 original candidates in CANDIDATES.md, each one line,
   each answering the wishes/howls/promises from the Moon's voice.
2. Picked the best: "I don't grant wishes. I just reflect sunlight
   and watch you all make the same mistakes in better lighting."
   (106 characters) — funny first, unmistakably the Moon's voice
   ("I just reflect sunlight"), twist in the tail ("in better
   lighting"), answers the wishes directly, quotable in one read.
3. main.py (stdlib only) writes exactly that line to entry.txt and
   re-reads it, asserting the gate (validate_entry) and the sha256
   digest, so any drift fails loudly.
4. Ran main.py twice; both runs byte-identical, sha256
   23507c0e7ffc374030a26d38d6d5612a26702a70cd53ca3be8f1c1259e53313b.
